Nourishing Food. Old Walls. Mindful Living.

Our dishes celebrate Appalachian traditions and Filipino-fusion flavors, made with local West Virginia ingredients—pawpaw, blackberries, ramps, kale, and beans grown in our rich mountain soil. Light, nourishing, and plant-forward, these are the foods that restore body and spirit around our farmhouse tables.
