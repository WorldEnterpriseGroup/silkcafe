Our Philosophy

Simple is Sophisticated

At SILK Cafe, we believe the most profound culinary experiences happen not in grand dining rooms, but in spaces that feel like home. Our Victorian cottages—each accommodating just 6 to 12 guests—become private stages where talented chefs practice their craft with rare intimacy.

This is not a restaurant. There are no walk-ins, no regular hours, no standard menus. Instead, our visiting chefs bring diverse culinary traditions—from Asian fusion to authentic Appalachian—creating singular experiences tailored exclusively to you.

  • Victorian Intimacy — Our 1880s cottage rooms seat 6-12 guests maximum
  • Visiting Culinary Masters — Talented chefs bringing diverse traditions to your table
  • Intentional Exclusivity — 2-4 weeks advance reservation required
  • Bespoke Menu Design — Every experience crafted to your preferences

Culinary Talent

Our Visiting Chefs

We work with talented chefs who bring diverse culinary traditions to our intimate cottage kitchen, each with their own story and expertise.

Asian Fusion

Chef Suzanne

Filipina Heritage

Chef Suzanne brings the bold, comforting flavors of Filipino cuisine to the Ohio Valley, reimagined with local ingredients. Her dishes honor family recipes passed down through generations while embracing the fresh produce and meats available from regional farms. Every meal tells a story of heritage meeting home.

Signature Dishes

Local shredded beef lumpia. Rice noodles with seasonal vegetables. Flavors that warm the soul.

Authentic Appalachian

Chef Dustin

Columbus, Ohio

With over 20 years of professional kitchen experience, Chef Dustin has dedicated his craft to elevating the honest, hearty cuisine of Appalachia. He sources from local farms and foragers, bringing the flavors of the region to the table with technique refined over two decades in kitchens across Ohio.

Signature Style

Root-to-stem cooking. Smoked meats. Preserved vegetables. The authentic taste of the hills.
