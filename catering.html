Community Access Point

Your Gateway to SILK Cafe Wholesome Food

SILK Cafe serves those who call SILK home—our intentional living community. Catering and pickup are how we share our simple, wholesome, home-style cooking with neighbors and friends beyond our walls, prepared fresh in our residential kitchens following cottage food law.

    Homemade Goodness

    Every order supports our intentional living community and connects you with our values of Strength, Integrity, Love, and Knowledge. Good food from good people, shared with intention.

Simple Process

How It Works

Getting wholesome food from SILK Cafe is easy. Order ahead, choose your pickup location, and enjoy.

  • Step 01

    Browse & Order

    Contact us to place your order: meal prep packages, catering for events, or cottage food items
    Pre-order required: All orders must be placed in advance to ensure fresh preparation

  • Step 02

    Choose Pickup Location

    Select from any of our 10 SILK Home locations in West Virginia and Ohio
    Catering delivery: Available for larger catering orders within our service area

  • Step 03

    Pick Up & Enjoy

    Pickup at your scheduled time from your chosen SILK Home location
    Fresh & ready: Prepared in our residential kitchens following cottage food law standards

Our Approach

Why Choose SILK Cafe Catering & Pickup?

Intentional by Design

  • SILK Cafe nourishes our members inside the homes
  • Catering & pickup let us welcome you to our table
  • All food follows cottage food law (WV/Ohio)
  • Prepared in residential-style kitchens at SILK Homes
  • Intentional Access

    Community Engagement by Design

    Inside our homes, the cafe serves the SILK family. Pickup and catering services invite neighbors to experience our food values while preserving the intimate, grounded nature of our intentional living spaces.

  • Home-Style Quality

    Not Commercial, That's the Point

    We use smart appliances and residential kitchens inside SILK Homes, not commercial equipment. This is simple, wholesome meal prep prepared with care—home cooking, not restaurant food. Compliance with cottage food law ensures quality and safety.
