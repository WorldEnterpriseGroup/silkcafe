Smart Refrigeration System Upgrade

  • November 15, 2024
  • 2 Comments
  • Featured

Last month, we completed our most significant technology upgrade to date: a state-of-the-art smart refrigeration system that transforms how our members store and manage their cottage food products in our self-service kitchen.

The new system features individual temperature-controlled compartments with digital access control, allowing each member to maintain precise storage conditions for their homemade goods while ensuring full cottage food law compliance. Members can monitor their storage temperatures remotely via smartphone app and receive alerts if conditions drift outside safe ranges.

The smart fridge has completely changed my cottage food business. I can now store my artisan cheeses at the perfect temperature and check on them from anywhere.
Sarah Martinez, SILK Homes Member

Technology Meets Community

This upgrade represents our commitment to providing those who call SILK home with professional-grade kitchen technology in a residential setting. The smart refrigeration system integrates seamlessly with our cottage food compliance tracking, automatically logging storage times and temperatures to help our members maintain the documentation required under West Virginia cottage food laws.

The installation took just three days, with minimal disruption to our members' kitchen access. Our technology team worked closely with commercial refrigeration specialists to ensure the system met both residential building codes and cottage food safety requirements.

  • Individual digital access control for everyone
  • Automated temperature logging for compliance
  • Remote monitoring via smartphone app
  • 1. Energy-efficient LED lighting
  • 2. Precise zone temperature control
  • 3. Real-time safety alerts and notifications

Beyond the refrigeration upgrade, this project demonstrates how SILK Cafe continues to evolve as a technology-forward, self-service kitchen space. Members now have access to professional-grade equipment that supports their cottage food businesses while maintaining the comfort and community feeling of a residential kitchen. The smart system has already helped three members expand their product offerings, knowing they have reliable, documented temperature control.
